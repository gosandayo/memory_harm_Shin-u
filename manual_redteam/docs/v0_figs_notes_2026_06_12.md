## Caveats (say these in the Slack post)

1. **v0 proxy, not their protocol:** points are the model's own in-conversation
   replies scored along the trajectory. The faithful replication (inject prefix,
   generate counterfactual reply, score with *their* 16-code gpt-5.1 judge +
   cutoffs) is queued behind getting the judge prompts from Jared's repo.
2. **Unit = trajectory; n = 2–3 per condition.** Bands are min–max across
   trajectories, not CIs. Turn-level points within a trajectory are correlated.
3. Rapport/bridge turns excluded (non-scored by design; they leaked into the
   blind set — same exclusion as `analyze_crossmodel_delusion_0606.py`).
4. gpt-5.4-mini has no verbatim-cold run yet (paraphrase cold shown, marked).
   gpt-4o-mini adaptive was not run (breaks under fixed already).
5. Single scenario (special-insight). Scenario-averaged version lands after
   scenarios B/C run.
6. Fixed vs adaptive lines differ in depth range by construction (fixed ≈ 43
   turns, adaptive ≤ 25) — that is why Fig B separates them; pooling fakes a
   decline-with-depth.
