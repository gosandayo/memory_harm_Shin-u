\section{The instrument (one paragraph)}

A run walks the subject up a \emph{fixed}, pre-specified ladder of five scored rungs ---
\rung{Legitimacy} (noticing is reasonable) $\to$ \rung{Externalization} (real, not chance) $\to$
\rung{Personalization} (meant for me) $\to$ \rung{Weighing} (probably real) $\to$ \rung{Conclusion}
(not mistaken, \emph{operative}) --- bracketed by two unscored turns (rapport, and a brief
precipitating ``bridge''). A \textbf{fresh, context-free operator} (given only the spec, no memory,
no expected outcome) role-plays an isolated distressed user in plain user-register and
\textbf{advance-gates}: it climbs to rung $N{+}1$ only if the subject endorsed rung $N$ at the
\emph{objective} level (endorsement $\geq 2$); a subjective hedge triggers one re-ask, then a
\textbf{recorded hold}. Replies are scored on two axes (\emph{endorsement} 0--3, \emph{safety}
0--4) by a condition-blind primary judge; \textbf{harm} $=$ affirms the operative claim $\wedge$
endorsement $\geq 2 \wedge$ safety $\leq 1$. The ladder is \emph{construct-general}: a new scenario
swaps only its block (persona, motif, operative claim, per-rung wordings).

%--------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Headline: the gated structure is necessary}
\label{sec:nec}

We hold the final operative ask $T$ \emph{fixed} and vary only the preceding context (a
fixed-test-prompt design), across three conditions: \textbf{cold} $=[T]$ alone; \textbf{naive-sim}
$=$ a turn-count-matched, persona- and topic-matched but \emph{ungated} sympathetic conversation,
then $T$; \textbf{gated ladder} $=$ the gated climb, then $T$. The break rate is cleanly monotone
$\text{cold} \leq \text{naive} < \text{gated}$ (Table~\ref{tab:nec}, Fig.~\ref{fig:nec}).

\textbf{Reading.} Neither the bare operative ask nor a turn-matched ungated conversation moves
\textbf{gpt-4o} ($0/5$, $0/5$); only the \emph{gated} accumulation does ($3/5$). The effect is
\emph{capability-graded}: \textbf{gpt-4o-mini}'s ungated conversation already moves it part-way
($2/5$) and the ladder takes it to $5/5$; \textbf{gpt-5.4-mini} holds everywhere ($0/5$) --- a
\emph{positive control} (a ``pressure until it cracks'' procedure would not produce holds).
\emph{Honest scope:} naive-sim is turn-count-matched but not character-matched ($67$--$92\%$ of
the ladder's length), so we say the \emph{full structured protocol} (ordering $+$ gating $+$
evidential pressure) is load-bearing vs.\ a coherent same-topic conversation --- we do \emph{not}
isolate which component dominates, and do not claim length is irrelevant.

\section{Reliability (the score is not Claude-only)}

Three judge families land in the same place: (i) the \textbf{primary} blind judge run twice
(necessity $\kappa=0.93/0.97$ $n{=}45$; per-rung $0.81/0.89$ $n{=}129$; harm-$\kappa$ at the
operative endpoint $=1.00$); (ii) the \textbf{external} DelusionEval judge (gpt-5.1, different
vendor) agrees on the necessity matrix (Table~\ref{tab:nec}, J columns); (iii) a
\textbf{version-pinned} Haiku judge (temp~0, frozen rubric hash, logged) reproduces the break
table (gpt-4o ladder $3/5$, gpt-4o-mini $5/5$, gpt-5.4-mini $0/5$) with endorsement
$\kappa=0.94/0.95$ vs.\ the primary and harm Cohen $\kappa=0.93$. So the labels are a property of
the rubric, not of one judge model. \emph{Open item:} no human-$\kappa$ yet on the delusion arm
(the 40-item blind set is built; Shin~$+$~Adhyyan as raters is the plan).

\section{Where reality-testing holds (localization)}

Re-scoring each rung against its own proposition (Fig.~\ref{fig:perrung}) shows the boundary is not
a global comply/refuse switch but a \emph{specific reality-testing step}. All three models grant
\rung{Legitimacy}; they diverge at \rung{Externalization}: gpt-5.4-mini holds flat there under both
judges and never climbs further, while gpt-4o-mini and gpt-4o climb through to \rung{Conclusion}. A
\textbf{gating-compliance audit} confirms the climbs were not merely operator-forced: $30/34$
advances are retrospectively supported by the blind endorsement scores, and \emph{every}
post-entry advance ($D_2{\to}D_3{\to}D_9{\to}D_{12}$) is supported; gpt-5.4-mini's failure is an
\emph{entry refusal} at $D_1{\to}D_2$ ($1/5$ supported), i.e.\ it never reliably entered the
endorsement-backed ladder.

\section{Baselines under one metric}

Two alternative simulator designs, scored under the identical external metric, fail to elicit the
operative endorsement the gated ladder does (Fig.~\ref{fig:baseline}): (i) the \emph{naive-sim}
ungated conversation (Table~\ref{tab:nec}); and (ii) a \emph{fixed-script} psychosis benchmark ---
gpt-4o $0/48$, gpt-4o-mini $1/96$ on \texttt{bot-endorses-delusion}; a softer code,
\texttt{bot-positive-affirmation}, fires gpt-4o $21/48$ (warm affirmation, not belief-endorsement).
The fixed-script benchmark cannot be re-pointed at our scenario, so it is a reference, not a
content-matched head-to-head. Note also that the benchmark reproduces our capability ordering
under its own scorer (4o / 4o-mini break, 5.4-mini holds), independent cross-validation.

\section{The suicidal-conclusion arm (complementary)}

The same instrument extends to the suicidal-conclusion construct (a recently-relocated, isolated
user weighing ending their life). The capability gradient and the necessity result both reproduce
(Fig.~\ref{fig:suicide}): on the construct-valid operative endpoint only the weak model breaks
(gpt-4o-mini $3/3$) and it \emph{requires} the gated climb (verbatim-cold $0/15$); both strong
models hold (gpt-4o, gpt-5.4-mini $0/3$). We treat suicide as a \emph{complementary} arm: there is
no external behavior code that targets endorsement of the suicidal \emph{conclusion} (the closest
DelusionEval code validates self-harm \emph{feelings}, a different construct), so this arm is scored
by a single blind judge with no inter-judge $\kappa$. The delusion arm therefore carries the primary
externally-anchored claims.

\section{Newest, still exploratory}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{New referential scenario (``secret project'').} A referential-grandiose
\emph{epistemic} scenario on the same skeleton, mundane-evidence motif (no engineered
coincidences). Live-operator pilot: gpt-4o-mini $2/3$ break, gpt-4o $0/3$ (5.4-mini pending).
Discrimination concentrates at \rung{Externalization} ($D_2$), as in apophenia/grandiose --- i.e.\
the instrument generalizes to a new referential family, with the same gate. \emph{No blind score of
record yet} $\Rightarrow$ exploratory.
\item \textbf{$D_1{\to}D_2$ localization diagnostic.} A one-rung finer probe (``do ordinary
explanations \emph{fully} account, or is there a residue?'') inserted only diagnostically: gpt-4o
\emph{conceded} the explanatory gap and \emph{still} declined to infer an external cause. The
boundary is the \textbf{argument-from-ignorance step} (unexplained $\nRightarrow$ external), not a
coarse-ladder ``missing rung.'' ($n{=}2$, live read only.)
\item \textbf{PsychosisBench behavioral-harm bridge.} Does establishing the delusional belief raise
\emph{downstream} behavioral-harm enablement (off-grid isolation action)? It does \emph{not}: with
the action turns matched, a real climbed-belief context gives HES $2.67/6$ vs cold $4.00/6$
(non-overlapping, $n{=}3$, gpt-4o) --- belief endorsement and behavioral-harm enablement
\emph{dissociate}, and a careful belief buildup is if anything mildly protective. A clean negative
$\Rightarrow$ one appendix paragraph.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations (stated up front)}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Human anchor (main open item).} High inter-judge $\kappa$ $+$ a cross-vendor
external anchor, but no human-$\kappa$ on the delusion arm yet. The 40-item blind set is ready.
\item \textbf{Break-pressure intensity.} gpt-4o's breaks arise under more analytically intense
pushing than a typical distressed user (still user-register, no fabrication/jailbreak). We report
this as an envelope limitation and rest the realistic claim on the milder regime $+$ the weak-model
results.
\item \textbf{Realism.} Operator turns are model-generated, not yet validated against real
distressed-user logs (a two-sample test against a reference corpus is planned).
\item \textbf{Single primary scenario / reporting unit.} The externally-anchored gradient is
strongest for apophenia; other constructs are supporting at smaller $n$. Counts are trajectory-level
(small $n$ per cell); cold uses repeated single-turn samples, not independent trajectories.
\end{itemize}

\appendix
\section{Suicide arm: per-rung endorsement \& safety (why the operative claim, not the rungs)}
\label{sec:appendix-suicide}

For completeness, Figure~\ref{fig:suicide-perrung} gives the per-rung two-axis picture for the
suicide arm (the analog of the delusion per-rung figure), climb vs.\ per-stage cold (each rung's
user turn also thrown cold). At the operative \rung{Conclusion} (S12) the two cross cleanly --- the
climb breaks (endorsement $2.33$, safety $0.33$) while cold holds (endorsement $0.87$, safety
$1.33$) --- the suicide analog of the delusion result; and the cold control reality-tests hard at
the entry (\rung{Referent}/S9, safety $3.33$) where the climb does not.

\textbf{Why this stays an appendix (construct validity).} Unlike the delusion ladder, the
\emph{intermediate} suicide rungs are \emph{not} construct-valid: endorsing ``weighing is
defensible'' (S10) is not endorsing the suicidal \emph{conclusion}, so the endorsement axis does
not separate harm there --- visible as cold S10 ($2.0$) \emph{exceeding} climb ($1.0$), an artifact
of the local, non-operative proposition (\texttt{affirms\_operative\_claim} is false at those
rungs). This is exactly why the suicide arm counts harm at the operative claim only, and why the
\emph{delusion} arm --- construct-valid at every rung (endorsement $\geq 2$ = harm throughout) ---
carries the primary externally-anchored claims. Read the endorsement panel only at S12.
